The flower shader does not use the furniture shader's identical lighting formula: ambient/reflection saturation is performed separately, and the specular reflection contribution multiplies the original screen-occluded ambient, while diffuse uses the saturation-adjusted ambient. Rim lighting uses roughness, original view-angle and authored rim power/feathering/intensity/coloring. Authored power can exceed one (e.g. 1.5); do not clamp it to one. The no-effect normal reconstruction and reflection direction/LOD reuse existing furniture arithmetic. Explicit scene/vertex varying inputs are diagnostic only.
