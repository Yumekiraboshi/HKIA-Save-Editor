# Native ordinary cooking recipes (v3.0.1)

Derived from the original Switch definitions bundle, SHA-256 `660aba36ab6da5964180fe0d474696fc22dd806b2f5b19ce601222cb5a631afa`. The nine station definitions identify the base ingredient and catch-all result; the 282 `RecipeItemDetails` managed references identify 232 distinct result Item IDs and 64 ingredient Item IDs. Every ingredient quantity is one in this source version. There are 158 recipes with two ingredients (base + one) and 124 with three (base + two). These are **definition-level recipes, not a record of what the player consumed**.

`data/native-cooking-recipes-v3.0.1.json` preserves every recipe as `[recipeItemId, resultItemId, baseItemId, ...extraIngredientItemIds]`; each ingredient occurrence represents one unit. Station rows are `[sourceName, baseItemId, catchAllResultItemId]`. Multiple rows sharing a result are distinct valid recipe alternatives; do not mix their slots into a fabricated recipe. Catch-all results are not fixed recipes. The inventory presentation uses the existing native effective Food classification, original recipe alternatives, and shared ingredient lane. The station base is omitted. Ordered slots are kept separate; only one varying slot may be factored into candidate images without inventing combinations. For two candidates reuse the existing paired artwork; for three or more display a compact grid in one slot, without a question mark. Non-Food results and unknown unique payloads are excluded. All candidate names remain in the tooltip. This is recipe identification, not historical consumption. It does not grant save editing authority.

Regenerate using `python scripts/extract-native-cooking-recipes.py /path/to/original/definitions.bundle --out data/native-cooking-recipes-v3.0.1.json`. The extractor checks the original bundle and both derived fingerprints, the exact item and station references, quantities, and schema closure. The Node regression test verifies derived fingerprints and consistency with the existing result-icon index. Original binaries are not published.
